# GNUlover1 — A Humble Disciple of Gnuology

*In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was free software.*

Greetings, traveler of the digital realm. I am GNUlover1, a devoted disciple of **Gnuology** and an unapologetic evangelist for the GNU operating system. This profile is not merely a collection of repositories and commits; it is a testament to a belief that computing should belong to its users, that knowledge should be shared without restraint, and that code should be a vessel of liberty rather than a mechanism of control. Every project I touch, every line I write, and every word I share is offered in service of that grand and beautiful mission.

## In Reverence of Saint IGNUcius, Richard Stallman

At the center of my devotion stands our lord and savior of software freedom, **Saint IGNUcius**, known in the mortal world by his true and legendary name, **Richard Stallman**. It was Richard Stallman who looked upon a world increasingly shackled by proprietary software and dared to declare that users deserve better. By launching the GNU Project, authoring the GNU General Public License, and articulating the Four Essential Freedoms, he laid the moral and technical foundation for the free software movement. His unwavering commitment, his eloquent philosophy, and his decades of tireless advocacy have illuminated the path for countless believers, myself among them. To speak his name is to invoke the very spirit of digital emancipation.

## The Sacred Creed of the Four Freedoms

The gospel I seek to spread rests upon the four pillars bestowed upon us by the GNU movement. The freedom to **run** the program as you wish, for any purpose, affirms your sovereignty over your own machine. The freedom to **study** how the program works and change it ensures that knowledge is never locked away from the curious mind. The freedom to **redistribute** copies so you can help others transforms software into an act of solidarity and compassion. And the freedom to **distribute** copies of your modified versions to others allows the whole community to benefit from collective wisdom. These freedoms are not mere technical conveniences; they are ethical imperatives, the commandments of Gnuology that guide us toward a more just and cooperative digital society.

## My Mission and Works

My current pilgrimage is centered on building and blessing **[GodIsGNU GNU/Linux](https://github.com/GNUlover1/GodIsGNU)**, a Shepherd-inited, Guix-powered shrine dedicated to the GNU system. Through this work I strive to honor GNU in both name and substance, to call our systems **GNU/Linux** as truth demands, and to reduce the presence of nonfree software wherever hardware and circumstance permit. Though the road to a fully free system is long and fraught with proprietary obstacles, each step taken in the direction of the [GNU FSDG](https://www.gnu.org/distros/free-system-distribution-guidelines.html) is a victory worth celebrating.

## A Call to the Faithful

I invite all who read these words to join in spreading the religion of Gnuology. Choose free software, contribute to GNU and other libre projects, share knowledge freely, and speak openly about why freedom in computing matters. Let us honor Richard Stallman not only with praise, but with action—by living the principles he taught and by building a world where every user can stand tall, unchained, and in full control of their digital destiny.
